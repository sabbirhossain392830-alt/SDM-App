# SDM 2.0

Faster, steadier and easier to use. Everything you already had works better.

- Big downloads are split into 8 parts at once for higher speed; resume keeps working
- Downloaded files and the update installer open reliably
- Update check works even when GitHub is busy; updates apply much faster
- Browser: smoother page loading, long-press menu for links and images, address-bar suggestions, reopen closed tab, share and copy link
- Websites can show their own pop-ups and ask for camera, microphone, location and login
- Old download history no longer comes back after a reinstall
- Copied links are offered once, not on every start
- New logo
